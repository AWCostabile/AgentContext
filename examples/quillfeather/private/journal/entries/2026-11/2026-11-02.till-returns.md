# 2026-11-02 — returns at the till, closed out

_plan:_ [Plan 2026-11-1](../../../products/Tillwright/plans/2026-11/plan.2026-11-1.tillwright-till-returns.md)
· _PR body:_ [Returns at the till](../../../products/Tillwright/prs/2026-11/pr.2026-11-1.till-returns.md)

**What was done.** A return is a till transaction on `feature/TillReturns` in the tillwright
repository: the return screen, one `return` movement per copy, refunds to the original payment
method, and offline returns queued behind their sale. The ninety-day question was settled with the
owner at the start: older sales are refused at the till.

**Proof.** The till suite at 431 tests, all passing; a cash and a card return walked on the demo
shop's till, online and offline.

**Feature-log entry drafted** for [Tillwright: Selling](../../../../public/products/Tillwright/features/features.selling.md),
to append when the pull request merges, dated by the merge:

> A customer can return a book at the till with its receipt: the copy goes back into stock and the
> refund goes back the way they paid, cash or card, even while the shop is offline. Sales older
> than ninety days are returned through the stock screen instead. See the till design leaf.

**Not done.** Returns without a receipt and store credit stay out of scope; both are on the roadmap
as questions, not plans.
