# Spinecard: Cards

_reviewed:_ 2026-11-02

**State today.** Staff write a recommendation of up to 280 characters for a title the shop holds
in Tillwright, and Spinecard prints it eight to a sheet of plain card stock with the title,
author, price and the printing date. Longer text is refused rather than cut. How it works:
[the card and its sheet](../design/design.card-layout.md); why:
[the card-printing rulings](../history/rulings.card-printing.md).

## 2026-W44 · Oct 26–Nov 1

**2026-10-29** — Cards now print the price with the date it was read from Tillwright, so a card
whose price may be out of date is visible on the shelf and in the card list, which sorts by that
date. See [the card and its sheet](../design/design.card-layout.md). Spinecard #4.
