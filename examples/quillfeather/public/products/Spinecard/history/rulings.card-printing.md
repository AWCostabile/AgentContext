# Rulings: printing a card

Ruled on 2026-10-20, before the first shops printed cards in volume. The question was what a card
may say and where its words come from.

- **CARD1.** A card is printed for one title the shop's Tillwright already holds. Spinecard reads
  the title, author and price from Tillwright and never keeps its own copy.
- **CARD2.** A card carries a recommendation of at most 280 characters, written by a staff member
  and signed with their first name. The card is refused, not cut, when the text is longer.
- **CARD3.** Cards print eight to a sheet of plain card stock, cut lines included. No special
  paper and no label printer.
- **CARD4.** The price on a card is the price at the moment of printing, and the card prints the
  date beside it, so a stale card is visible on the shelf.
