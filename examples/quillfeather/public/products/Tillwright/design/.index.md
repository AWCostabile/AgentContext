# Tillwright design

Current law: how it works today in Tillwright and what was ruled. One subject per leaf
(`design.<subsystem>.md`), at most about 120 lines. Under its H1 a leaf names the rulings it rests on
and the date someone last confirmed it. When a leaf contradicts `history/`, the leaf wins and says what
it supersedes and why.
