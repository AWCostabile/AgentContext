# Inbox

The one cheap landing for a thought that arrives mid-session. Append only, one item per line,
`- YYYY-MM-DD — <text>`. Under **Mine**, the user's own captures; under **Candidates**, what an
agent proposes should become a ruling, a design change or a feature-log line, for the user to
promote or drop. An item leaves by being promoted (filed properly: a leaf with its row and tags,
a ruling, a design change, a feature-log entry) or dropped; nothing here is filed by an agent on
its own. An item older than 14 days is a smell (S21).
