# Products — private mirror

The private mirror of the products catalog: how the work on each of its products was run, the
development-cycle records. What is true of a product lives in the public branch of the same name:
[Products](../../public/products/.index.md). Copy `common/templates/product-mirror` here once per
product, under the same path `public/` uses (`<catalog>/<Name>/`), and fill in every placeholder.
