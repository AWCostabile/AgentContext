# Winchbook plans

Implementation briefs, grouped by month (`YYYY-MM/`, keyed on the Created date of each leaf). A plan
is editable while its "Landed as" is `—`; landed, it is frozen. Each plan is numbered within its
month: `plan.<YYYY-MM>-<n>.<product>-<slug>.md`, `<n>` one counter per month across all products,
restarting at 1 and repeated in the H1. A plan's kickoff, PR body, appendices and closing journal
entry all file under the month it was initiated in. A new month's first filing creates its branch.
