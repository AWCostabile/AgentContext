# Rulings

Enacted law about the tree itself and its governing process, immutable once adopted: each record
added verbatim with a manifest row. Seven of the template's eight seed leaves were adopted
on 2026-11-04, every ruling a stable id. The agents-and-budgets leaf was not: how a delegated agent
works is proposed afresh in the constitution's first bill. CP12 was struck and WK10 amended
before the status lines were set; the status line is what freezes a leaf. After adoption every
change is a bill, enacted as a new leaf.
