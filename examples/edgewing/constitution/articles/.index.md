# Articles

Current law for the tree itself: one article per subject, each citing the seed rulings it rests
on (`_source:_`, under `history/rulings/`) and saying what it supersedes; the design tier under
its constitutional name. An article cites law, or an open bill pending enactment; never an
enacted or withdrawn bill. Delete an article whose rulings an instance did not adopt.
