# Bills

Prospective law: proposals, one bill per plan, `bill.<YYYY-MM>-<n>.<slug>.md`, filed before the work
starts, citable from the kickoff on; amendment is in place, each correction with a dated
`_amended YYYY-MM-DD:_` line. At enactment the owner has it copied verbatim into `history/rulings/` as
`rulings.<slug>.md`, or it is withdrawn. Rows newest first.
