# Rulings: the launch line

Ruled on 2026-10-14, after two clubs ran a flying weekend on paper beside the first tablet. The
question was what the queue at the launch point holds, and when a flight enters the log.

- **LINE1.** The queue keeps one line per launch method in use that day, winch and aerotow; a
  club that names none runs a winch line only.
- **LINE2.** A glider waits at most once in a line. A second join is refused and the refusal is
  shown; a glider may wait in both lines at once.
- **LINE3.** A flight enters the log at its launch, never before: the front slot of a line becomes
  an airborne entry stamped with the launch time.
- **LINE4.** A landing closes the newest airborne entry for its glider. A landing with no airborne
  entry is refused.
- **LINE5.** A flight's length is whole minutes, rounded down; an airborne flight has no length.
- **LINE6.** The queue and the log live on the launch-point tablet and never wait on a network.
