# Winchbook: Launching

_reviewed:_ 2026-11-04

**State today.** The launch-point tablet keeps one queue line per launch method the club runs
that day, winch, aerotow or both, and a club that names none gets a winch line. A glider joins at
the back of a line; a second join of the same glider to the same line is refused on screen, and a
glider may wait in both lines at once. The winch driver or the tug pilot takes the front of their
own line. How it works: [the launch line and the day's log](../design/design.launch-line.md);
why: [the launch-line rulings](../history/rulings.launch-line.md).

## 2026-W44 · Oct 26–Nov 1

**2026-10-29** — A club can run an aerotow line beside the winch line, each launching from its
own front, and a glider may wait in both. See [the launch line and the day's log](../design/design.launch-line.md).
Winchbook #3.

## 2026-W43 · Oct 19–25

**2026-10-22** — The winch queue refuses a glider already waiting in it and says so, so a double
tap never queues one glider twice. See [the launch-line rulings](../history/rulings.launch-line.md).
Winchbook #2.
