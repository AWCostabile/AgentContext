# Winchbook: Flight log

_reviewed:_ 2026-11-04

**State today.** A flight enters the day's log at its launch, as an airborne entry stamped with
the launch time, and a landing closes the newest airborne entry for that glider. Each landed
flight shows its length in whole minutes. The log lives on the tablet and needs no signal at the
launch point. How it works: [the launch line and the day's log](../design/design.launch-line.md);
why: [the launch-line rulings](../history/rulings.launch-line.md).

## 2026-W43 · Oct 19–25

**2026-10-22** — Launching a glider writes its airborne entry into the day's log, and its landing
closes it with the flight's length in whole minutes; a landing for a glider that is not airborne
is refused. See [the launch line and the day's log](../design/design.launch-line.md). Winchbook #2.
