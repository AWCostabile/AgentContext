# Winchbook glossary

One entry per term, as each is settled; a term's law lives in the design leaf it links to.
Keep the list alphabetical, one line per term, and strike a term here when the leaf that
holds its law is archived.

- **aerotow** — a launch behind a tow plane, the tug; its gliders wait in the aerotow line. See [the launch line and the day's log](./design/design.launch-line.md).
- **airborne entry** — a log entry with a launch time and no landing yet; it has no flight length. See [the launch line and the day's log](./design/design.launch-line.md).
- **launch point** — the end of the field where gliders wait and are launched, and where the tablet sits. See [the launch line and the day's log](./design/design.launch-line.md).
- **line** — the queue of one launch method for the day, launched from its front. See [the launch line and the day's log](./design/design.launch-line.md).
- **slot** — one glider waiting in a line: its registration, its pilot, the method, and an instructor when it is a lesson. See [the launch line and the day's log](./design/design.launch-line.md).
- **winch** — a launch by a cable wound in fast from the far end of the field; the default line. See [the launch line and the day's log](./design/design.launch-line.md).
