# Rulings

Enacted law about the tree itself and its governing process, immutable once adopted: each record
added verbatim with a manifest row. The eight seed leaves are the template's starting law, one
per theme, every ruling a stable id. Prune at adoption: delete what does not apply, amend what
does, then set the leaf's status to `_status:_ adopted YYYY-MM-DD` and its Created to that date;
the status line is what freezes a leaf. After adoption every change is a bill, enacted as a new leaf.
