# Constitution

The third tree, beside `public/` and `private/`: the tree's law about itself and the governance that
keeps the work honest, in the same branch grammar. Law has three tiers here: bills (prospective),
rulings (enacted, immutable) and articles (current law, citing the rulings). Read an article for
what holds; its rulings for why; a bill for what is proposed. The operative files that enforce it
stay where the tooling loads them and each cites its article.

## Contents
