# Journal

The work ledger of this machine. [Latest](./latest.md) is the status page (the active work): in flux,
last landed, next open, unpushed branches. It is rewritten as work moves and stays at the root. Five
branches hold the rest. A new month's first filing creates its month branch (`YYYY-MM/`, with its own
`.index.md` and `.tags`) and its row in the parent's Contents.
