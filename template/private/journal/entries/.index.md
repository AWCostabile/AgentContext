# Journal Entries

The dated landing entries (`<date>.<slug>.md`): what a change did, where, the proof, and the
feature-log entries drafted for it. Appended as work lands and never edited, grouped by month
(`YYYY-MM/`). The entry that closes a plan files under the plan's month, however long it ran.
