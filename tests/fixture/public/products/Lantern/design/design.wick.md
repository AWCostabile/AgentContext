# The wick

_source:_ public/products/Lantern/history/rulings.wick.md W1–W2; public/products/Lantern/history/rulings.flame.md F1
_reviewed:_ 2026-11-01

The wick is trimmed to a quarter inch before lighting and lit from the side, never the top.

- A frayed wick is replaced, never trimmed again.
- The flame height follows the flame ruling.
